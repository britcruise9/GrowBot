GrowBot does not move the arm with today's arm_body.py, so this page is the only way (a new arm file that keeps parts holding is being tested). Close GrowBot while you use it: GrowBot sends a stop when it starts most leg moves and when you stop it, and a stop lets the arm go.

joints

Each joint holds still after a move, then lets go after 2 minutes with no new move, or 15 s after this page closes, sleeps or loses its connection.

poses

STOP stops everything: the arm and the wheels or legs. Leaving this page lets the arm go too.
slider limits (this browser)
The robot clamps every move to the limits in its arm_config.py anyway. Narrow the sliders here while you find each joint's safe range.
