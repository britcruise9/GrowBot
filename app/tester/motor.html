① Check the legs

Run the 3 steps if something looks off. (Advanced is only needed if a sweep goes backward.)
Step 1 · which leg
The build guide wiring is the default: no swap needed. LEFT and RIGHT are the robot's sides, so LEFT is on your right when you face its screen. Tap each button and check which leg moves; ignore the movement direction.
Troubleshooting: left and right connections reversed
Use this only if LEFT moves the right leg and RIGHT moves the left leg. Walking backward does not mean the connections are reversed. Existing saved settings are kept. Screen-forward walking currently requires standard wiring with swap off.
Step 2 · straighten
Trim until both legs hang straight down.
L trim0
R trim0
Step 3 · direction
Tap a sweep — the leg should sweep DOWN toward the screen (that is forward). If it lifts UP or sweeps backward instead, open Advanced and flip that leg.
